How it works

From request to confirmed care, in three steps.

1

Submit a request

Families provide care requirements, location, schedule, and preferences through a structured request form.

2

Receive matches

The matching engine ranks suitable, verified caregivers by skills, location, and availability.

3

Book with confidence

Track the service lifecycle from confirmation to completion, then leave feedback for future families.

About Avitacare

A structured alternative to informal caregiver sourcing.

Avitacare connects families with verified, qualified caregivers through structured profiles, criteria based matching, and a transparent booking lifecycle, replacing word of mouth referrals with accountable, trackable coordination.

Document based verification for every caregiver
Structured requests matched on skills and location
Reviews tied to completed bookings, not self reported
Full booking lifecycle, tracked end to end
