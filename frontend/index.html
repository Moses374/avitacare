How AvitaCare works

A simple, structured process from search to care delivery.

01
Create your profile

Register as a family or caregiver. Families describe their care needs. Caregivers list their qualifications, experience, and availability.

02
Get matched

Families submit a structured service request. Our system surfaces caregivers whose skills, location, and availability align with your needs.

03
Book and manage

Confirm your caregiver, manage the booking through the platform, and leave a review when the engagement is complete.

Why families and caregivers choose AvitaCare

Verified caregivers

Every caregiver profile is reviewed by our administration team before going live. Families can see verified qualification summaries directly on each profile.

Structured matching

Service requests are matched against caregiver profiles based on care type, location, and availability — not informal referrals or word of mouth.

Full booking lifecycle

From request to completion, every engagement is tracked on the platform. Both parties have a clear record of what was agreed and when.

Transparent reviews

After each completed booking, families leave a rating and written review. Caregivers build a public reputation that reflects their actual performance.
